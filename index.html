01 / Overview

Practical skills. Real examples. Better decisions.

AI is changing how professional work gets researched, communicated, designed, and delivered. This conference connects basic skills with the decisions that matter inside a real practice: choosing tools, checking results, protecting information, and deciding where automation is useful.

Daniel and Jenn will draw on their work in digital design, visualization, and AI education to connect everyday tasks with more complex workflows. Guest sessions will extend the discussion into academia, specialist applications, and advanced agents.

Who should attend?

For people doing the work and people leading the practice. Whether you are trying AI for the first time or deciding how to introduce it across a team, the program moves from accessible fundamentals to more advanced applications and business decisions.

Landscape architects, architects, planners, designers, visualization professionals, educators, firm owners, and practice leaders.

Our approach

Start simply. Build context. Test the result. Keep people accountable.

Sessions connect demonstrations to the professional judgment needed to use the output. Case studies should show the task, inputs, process, result, review, and limitations—not just a polished final image.

What you will learn

  1. Understand what a large language model is, what it can do, and why its answers need checking.
  2. Write a useful prompt and improve it through context, examples, constraints, and iteration.
  3. Compare AI tools by task, privacy needs, source quality, cost, and workflow fit.
  4. Use deeper research workflows and assess the evidence behind a response.
  5. Apply AI to writing, proposals, 2D imagery, 3D workflows, modeling, rendering, and coding.
  6. Connect firm policy, responsible use, and human review to everyday practice.
  7. Examine the infrastructure, energy, and growth questions behind AI adoption.
  8. Understand agents and identify a practical first step toward firm implementation.

Do I need previous AI experience?

No. The program begins with basic concepts and simple prompts before moving into more advanced workflows and firm-level decisions.

Is this only for designers?

The examples draw heavily on design and professional practice, while research, writing, policy, and implementation sessions apply across many organizations.

Is this a hands-on workshop?

The proposed format combines presentations, demonstrations, case studies, and questions. Any guided exercises or software requirements will be announced with the final program.

Laptop and account requirements are not finalized. Do not purchase a subscription specifically for the event until requirements are published.
